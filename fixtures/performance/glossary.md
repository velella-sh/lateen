The glossary turns a collection's records into an alphabetical page, and a term in running text
opens its definition in place.

::glossary

A :term[Widget] is composed into a page. A :term[control]{id=widget} reaches the same entry by
another name, and an unknown :term[nosuch] stays as written with a marker.
