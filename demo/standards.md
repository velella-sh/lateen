## Principles

Sailcloth means quiet Foam and Night fields, Lateen Blue for identity and links, and Mist as a whisper. Chrome is transparent at rest and glasses after scroll. Surfaces stay free of colored frames.

## Page anatomy

Doc pages use three columns: side nav, article, and on-this-page TOC. Home uses a two-column stage with a docs preview, then section cards.

## Definition of done

A page is done when the H1 and dek state the promise, every H2 is in the TOC, commands are copyable, and light and dark both keep contrast.
